# Grants & Financial Assistance

Listed in order of most accessible to least. Start with the top 3 — they are free and respond fastest.

---

## 1. SCORE Mentorship — Free

**Cost:** $0  
**What it is:** Free mentorship from retired business executives. They help with business plans, financials, loan packaging, and connecting to local resources. East Valley chapter is active.  
**How to apply:** score.org → "Find a Mentor" → select Mesa/Chandler location  
**Timeline:** Match within 1–2 weeks. Ongoing free meetings.

---

## 2. Arizona Small Business Development Center (AZ SBDC)

**Cost:** Free advising  
**What it is:** Free one-on-one consulting at Mesa Community College campus. They will help you build the business plan, project cash flow, and prepare a loan application.  
**Contact:** mesacc.edu/sbdc or (480) 784-0590  
**Timeline:** Appointment within 1–2 weeks.

---

## 3. Prestamos CDFI (SBA Microloan)

**Amount:** Up to $50,000  
**What it is:** SBA-backed microlender focused on Latino entrepreneurs in AZ. Flexible underwriting — works with new businesses and people without perfect credit. Lower rates than commercial banks.  
**Contact:** prestamosloans.org | (602) 252-1822  
**Requirements:** Business plan, cash flow projections, personal ID. They will help you prepare if needed.  
**Timeline:** Decision in 2–4 weeks after complete application.

---

## 4. SBA Microloan Program (via local intermediary)

**Amount:** Up to $50,000  
**What it is:** SBA provides funds to non-profit intermediaries (like Prestamos) who lend to small businesses. Interest typically 8–13%. No collateral required for smaller amounts.  
**How to access:** Through Prestamos or other AZ SBA intermediaries. Find at sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/microloans.

---

## 5. City of Mesa Small Business Grant

**Amount:** Varies — check mesaaz.gov for open rounds  
**What it is:** Mesa periodically opens small business grant rounds, sometimes focused on underserved communities or specific districts.  
**Contact:** Mesa Economic Development, (480) 644-3233, mesaaz.gov/business

---

## 6. Arizona Community Foundation — Small Business Grants

**Amount:** Typically $2,500–$10,000  
**What it is:** Various grant programs, some focused on minority-owned businesses and food entrepreneurs.  
**Contact:** azfoundation.org  
**Tip:** Check the AZ Hispanic Chamber (azhcc.com) for grant listings specifically for Hispanic entrepreneurs.

---

## 7. AZ Hispanic Chamber of Commerce

**Amount:** Varies — access to lenders + grant alerts  
**What it is:** Connects Hispanic business owners to lenders, grants, and networking. They often know about grant windows before they are widely publicized.  
**Contact:** azhcc.com | (602) 252-1022

---

> **Fastest path to money:** Call Prestamos first + book an SBDC appointment simultaneously. Have your menu, truck info, and a rough monthly expense estimate ready for both calls.
