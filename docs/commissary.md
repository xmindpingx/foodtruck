# Commissary Options — Mesa / East Valley

Maricopa County requires daily reporting to a licensed commissary. You must have a signed Commissary Agreement before your county permit is issued.

> **Requirement:** Commissary must be a Maricopa County-permitted food establishment. They must sign the county Commissary Agreement form and provide their permit number. Call to confirm current availability and pricing before visiting.

---

## Option 1 — BLT Kitchens East Mesa

**Location:** Power Rd & Southern Ave area, Mesa, AZ  
**Type:** Shared commercial kitchen / commissary built for food trucks  
**Services likely offered:** Water fill, gray water dump, storage, walk-in cooler access, prep space  
**Action:** Search "BLT Kitchens Mesa" — verify they are currently accepting food truck commissary clients and confirm their Maricopa County permit number  

---

## Option 2 — Our Community Kitchen

**Location:** 2655 W. Guadalupe Rd, Mesa, AZ 85202  
**Type:** Non-profit shared commercial kitchen  
**Why it fits:** Typically lower rates than private commissaries; mission-aligned with small food businesses; may have flexible scheduling  
**Action:** Call ahead — confirm they service mobile food trucks as commissary clients and can sign the Maricopa County agreement  

---

## Option 3 — Local Restaurant (approach independently)

Any Maricopa County-permitted restaurant can serve as your commissary if they agree to provide water fill, dump, and storage access. Many restaurants do this for a monthly fee.

**What to say:**  
> "Are you willing to sign a Maricopa County Commissary Agreement as our commissary location? We need daily water fill, gray water dump, and storage access."

**Tip:** Try Hispanic restaurants that understand food truck culture and may want to support a new business in the community.

---

## What the Commissary Agreement Form Requires

| Field | What It Means |
|-------|---------------|
| Commissary name + permit number | The licensed facility's county permit — verify it is current |
| Services provided | Water fill, gray water dump, storage, equipment washing — all you actually use |
| Authorized commissary signature | Owner or manager of the commissary must sign |
| Daily reporting log | You or employee signs in at commissary each operating day |
