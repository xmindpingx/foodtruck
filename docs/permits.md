# Permitting Roadmap

Complete these steps in order. Do not skip ahead — county plan review must happen before you operate.

---

## Step 1 — Form Your LLC
**Fee:** $50 AZ ACC filing fee  
File online at azcc.gov → Corporations Division. Takes 3–5 business days.  
Get your EIN from IRS.gov — free and instant (call or online).  
Get both before applying for any license.

## Step 2 — Arizona TPT License
**Fee:** $12 state fee  
Apply at AZTaxes.gov. Select "Restaurant" under retail.  
Combined Mesa rate = 8.30% on food sales (effective Jan 1, 2026).  
Must have this before the city license.

## Step 3 — Food Handler Cards (all food workers)
**Fee:** ~$15–25 per person  
Maricopa County requires a Food Handler Card for everyone who handles food.  
Take the online course at foodhandlers.com or maricopa.gov/eh (~90 min).  
The Certified Food Manager (ServSafe or equivalent) is required for the permit holder.  
ServSafe exam: $15 online or ~$36 in-person.

## Step 4 — Sign Commissary Agreement
**Fee:** $0 form fee (commissary rental separate)  
Maricopa County requires a signed Commissary Agreement before issuing a mobile food permit.  
Must be an inspected, licensed commissary. See `docs/commissary.md` for options.  
Get the agreement signed before submitting county paperwork.

## Step 5 — Maricopa County Mobile Food Permit (Plan Review)
**Fee:** $165 new/change-of-ownership  
Submit to: Maricopa County Environmental Services (EHS), maricopa.gov/ehs  
Phone: (602) 506-6616  

**Submit with application:**
- Commissary agreement
- Menu
- Equipment list with NSF numbers
- Floor plan sketch of truck with equipment placement
- Proof of Certified Food Manager

You are a Type III unit (cooking from raw on truck).

## Step 6 — Maricopa County On-Site Health Inspection
**Fee:** Included in $165  
Inspector comes to the truck. All MUST FIX items from `docs/repairs.md` must be complete.  

**Inspector checks:**
- Food temps (fridge ≤41°F, cooked meat ≥165°F)
- Equipment NSF-listed
- No wood surfaces
- Handwash sink (dedicated, with soap and paper towels)
- 3-compartment sink
- Hot and cold running water
- Pest exclusion screens
- Service window self-closing cover
- Clean interior

Pass = county permit issued.

## Step 7 — Mesa Mobile Food Vendor License
**Fee:** $50 initial + $20/yr renewal  
Apply at Mesa City Hall, 20 E. Main St., or online at mesaaz.gov  
Phone: (480) 644-2351  

**Bring:**
- County permit
- TPT license
- LLC docs
- EIN confirmation

Note: HB2118 that would have eliminated this license was vetoed June 19, 2026. Mesa still requires it.

## Step 8 — Mesa Fire Department Inspection
**Fee:** Call for current fee — (480) 644-2642  
Required per 2018 IFC Section 319 for mobile food units with cooking equipment.  

**Inspector checks:**
- Fire suppression system (K-Class, UL300 listed)
- Cooking equipment clearances
- LP gas connections (if gas)
- Electrical
- Ventilation hood

Schedule AFTER county permit is in hand.  
Have fire suppression system serviced first — tag must show service within 6 months.

## Step 9 — AZ Withholding Registration (if paying employees)
**Fee:** $0  
Register at AZTaxes.gov for employer withholding tax.  
If only you and family, may not apply — confirm with your CPA.
