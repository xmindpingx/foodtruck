ABOUT MY OPERATION

  Menu: Authentic Hispanic street food (burritos, tacos, tostadas, chorizo/eggs)
  Type III mobile food unit (cooking raw on vehicle)
  Planned service days: [DAYS/WEEK, e.g., Tue–Sat]
  Estimated start date: [DATE]

  - Fresh water fill (potable)
  - Wastewater dump (gray water)

Could we schedule a brief call or visit to discuss rates and availability?

Thank you,
[YOUR NAME]
[LLC NAME]
[PHONE] | [EMAIL]
