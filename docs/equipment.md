# Equipment & Supplies Buy List

Everything not confirmed present on the truck. NSF-listed items required by Maricopa County. Quantities based on 50-order-per-shift Hispanic menu (burritos, tacos, tostadas, chorizo+eggs).

---

## Cooking — Core

| Item | Spec | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Commercial Griddle | 36" or 48" wide; NSF-listed; gas or propane; thermostatic control | Primary cook surface for chorizo, eggs, meat |
| Countertop Range / Burner | 2-burner commercial (NSF); 60,000–90,000 BTU | Heating beans, rice, sauces, water for sanitation |
| Commercial Hood + Exhaust Fan | Must cover all cooking equipment; Type I (grease-rated); UL-listed | Required by fire code over any open flame or high-heat cooking |
| K-Class Fire Suppression System | UL 300 listed; must cover hood; serviced within 6 months (tag required) | Required by Mesa Fire IFC 319 for cooking with grease |
| K-Class Portable Fire Extinguisher | Minimum 1.5 gallon K-class; wall-mounted in accessible location | Required in addition to suppression system |

## Refrigeration / Food Holding

| Item | Spec | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Undercounter Refrigerator | NSF-listed; holds 33–38°F; min 5 cu ft; for raw meat/eggs | Raw animal proteins must be stored below ready-to-eat food |
| Prep Cooler / Cold Rail Insert | 36"–48" refrigerated prep rail, NSF; holds toppings at 41°F or below | For salsa, pico, cheese, sour cream, guac at service line |
| Hotel Pans (1/3 size × 6) | Stainless, NSF; 4" depth; lids for each | Toppings: pico, onion/cilantro, cheese, sour cream, jalapeños, salsa |
| Hotel Pans (1/6 size × 4) | Stainless, NSF; 4" depth | Smaller batch items: chorizo, guacamole, lime wedges |
| Food-Grade Containers w/ Lids (6 qt × 6) | Cambro or equivalent; NSF; clear or white; label + date tape | Prepped salsas, marinated meats, cooked beans for transport from commissary |
| Squeeze Bottles (8 × 12 oz) | NSF; wide-mouth; labeled | Salsa roja, salsa verde, crema, hot sauce at service |

## Water / Sanitation Systems

| Item | Spec | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| 3-Compartment Sink | NSF; minimum 3 compartments each 10"×14"×10" deep; with drain boards | Required by county for wash/rinse/sanitize cycle |
| Handwashing Sink (dedicated) | NSF; separate from 3-compartment; self-contained or plumbed; with paper towels + soap | Required by county — a dedicated handwash sink is non-negotiable |
| Fresh Water Tank | Minimum 30 gallons; NSF food-grade plastic; with pump or gravity feed | County requires sufficient potable water for entire shift |
| Gray Water Tank | Minimum 15% larger than fresh water tank (min ~35 gal); sealed | County requires captured gray water — cannot dump on ground |
| Water Heater (on-demand) | Propane or 12V demand water heater; must deliver 110°F minimum at handwash sink | County requires hot water at handwash sink |
| Sanitizer Test Strips | Chlorine and/or quaternary ammonium; 100–200 strips | Required to verify sanitizer concentration (50–100 ppm chlorine) |
| NSF Dish Soap | Commercial degreaser / dish detergent; food-safe | 3-comp sink wash step |
| Unscented Bleach / Sanitizer Concentrate | Food-safe chlorine sanitizer; 1 gal | 3-comp sanitize step and surface sanitizing |
| Hand Soap (liquid, unscented) | NSF or food-safe; dispenser at handwash sink | County inspects handwash sink for soap and towels |
| Paper Towels (roll × 4) | Standard; at handwash sink | Required at handwash sink — cloth towels not permitted |
