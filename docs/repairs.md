# Repair & Fix List

Based on photo review of the 1990 GMC step-van. Items are grouped by priority.

**MUST FIX** = blocks county health or Mesa fire inspection  
**BEFORE OPEN** = must be done before first day of service  
**SOON** = within 60 days of opening  

---

## ELECTRICAL — Cab & Chassis

### [MUST FIX] Exposed/loose wiring under cab dash
Hanging blue butt-connector wires and white plug visible under dashboard. Toggle switches have hand-written labels. Fire marshal will note any exposed conductors in the cab.  
**Action:** Route and loom all hanging wires; secure with wire ties; label switches with legible permanent labels. Verify all cab grounds are tight.

### [MUST FIX] Awning light panels — no lens covers
4 fluorescent tubes are bare (no diffuser/lens), dirty, and caulk around fixture is cracked/peeling.  
**Action:** Install replacement LED strip lights with weather-rated fixture covers (or swap tubes for LED strips + cover panels). Re-caulk mounting seam with food-grade silicone. Verify on GFCI circuit.

### [BEFORE OPEN] Speaker wiring
Head unit wiring harness photographed but speakers not connected.  
**Action:** Run speaker wire through loom alongside light wiring; connect to head-unit harness (Yellow=+12V, Black=GND, Red=ACC). Secure inside conduit so no bare wires visible in food area.

### [MUST FIX] Service window — gas strut loose wires
Red and blue wires taped along service-window gas strut; loose black wires with exposed butt connector hanging at window opening.  
**Action:** Secure all wires in loom or raceway away from food-contact surfaces and customer window opening.

### [MUST FIX] Frayed wire bundle — refrigeration controls
Frayed woven sleeve exposing colored conductors at refrigeration control panel (Ranco controller area).  
**Action:** Replace sleeve with split-loom conduit; secure all conductors; verify no bare copper exposed.

---

## BATTERY BANK

### [MUST FIX] Corroded battery terminals + broken tray
Heavy green/white corrosion on at least 2 terminals; broken battery-tray edge; no hold-down strap visible; debris in the tray.  
**Action:** Clean terminals with baking soda solution + wire brush; apply anti-corrosion spray; replace or repair broken tray; install hold-down strap (fire code requires secured batteries); remove all foreign debris.

### [BEFORE OPEN] Rusted junction box — refrigeration bay
Heavily rusted electrical box at refrigeration controls.  
**Action:** Open box; inspect terminals; treat or replace box if rust is through-wall; ensure IP-rated cover latches properly.

---

## REFRIGERATION UNIT

### [BEFORE OPEN] Compressor shell rust + performance verification
Condensing unit (AKA4460YXA hermetic compressor) has heavy rust on lower shell. Unit must hold food at 41°F or below.  
**Action:** Have a refrigeration tech check refrigerant charge; verify sight glass is clear (not bubbling); confirm unit holds 41°F or below. Replace filter-drier if last service unknown. Paint or treat shell rust.

---

## SERVICE WINDOW

### [MUST FIX] Plexiglass / barrier installation
Plexiglass is on order. County health code requires an approved barrier between food prep area and customers.  
**Action:** Install ordered plexiglass panels. Panel must be openable only from inside. Max opening 324 sq in per Maricopa County rules.

### [MUST FIX] Rusty hinges on service window panels
2 clearly rusted hinges on existing hinged plexi panels.  
**Action:** Replace with stainless steel hinges. Food-zone hardware must be non-corroding.

### [MUST FIX] Screen required on service window
No screen visible in photos. Maricopa County requires a screen on any opening to the exterior (pest exclusion).  
**Action:** Install 16-mesh or finer screen on service window. Magnetic or sliding screen works.

---

## INTERIOR FOOD AREA

### [MUST FIX] Griddle — rust/residue, wood board on counter
Griddle surface shows rust-colored residue. Dark wood plank on counter — wood is prohibited in food prep areas (porous, cannot be sanitized).  
**Action:** Deep-clean and re-season griddle; replace if rust is pitted through. Remove all wood items from food prep surfaces. Replace with stainless or NSF-listed material only.

### [MUST FIX] Personal items / non-food items in food cabinets
Personal drink cups, wallet-type item, and tools (rusted hinge brackets, fryer-basket handle, scraper) stored in food-area cabinets.  
**Action:** Remove all personal items. Designate separate locked storage for tools/personal items. Dedicate all stainless wells and cabinets to food-only use.

### [BEFORE OPEN] Stagnant water / residue in wells
Dirty water and residue visible in at least one well cover area; brown debris flakes in a stainless basin.  
**Action:** Full clean and sanitize of all wells and basins. Ensure drain hoses are functional and connected.

### [BEFORE OPEN] Peeling paint/adhesive on walls
Blue-painted wall around service window has peeling paint and adhesive residue.  
**Action:** Strip loose paint/adhesive; repaint with food-safe, light-colored, washable paint (or apply stainless or FRP panels). All interior walls in food zone must be smooth, light-colored, and easily cleanable.

### [MUST FIX] Bare copper wire coil in food area
Bare copper wire coil hanging on pipe inside the main food compartment.  
**Action:** Remove all loose wire from food prep area.

---

## EXTERIOR / VEHICLE

### [SOON] Rear bumper rust + body crack
Rust beside rear bumper bracket; crack in front body panel near amber turn signal.  
**Action:** Treat rust with converter and paint. Not an inspection blocker for food permit but required for DMV compliance.

### [BEFORE OPEN] Cracked side mirror glass
Driver-side mirror cracked (held with tape).  
**Action:** Replace mirror glass — AZ law requires functional mirrors.

### [SOON] Rusted driver seat mount bracket
Heavy rust on driver seat floor bracket.  
**Action:** Treat/repaint; verify bolts are secure.

### [SOON] Soot staining on rear stainless panel
Dark soot-like staining in rear compartment upper-right stainless panel.  
**Action:** Clean. If from exhaust/fire source, identify and correct source first.
