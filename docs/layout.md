# Station Layout — El Otro Lado

1990 GMC Step-Van · Interior approximately 14 ft long × 7 ft wide × 6 ft ceiling  
Single service window on passenger side. Rear doors.

> **Tape your truck first.** Before buying equipment, run masking tape on the counter marking where each station goes. Confirm the griddle + hood + suppression system all clear the ceiling. Verify the 3-comp and handwash sinks drain to your gray water tank.

## Zone Details

### Zone 1 — Handwash Sink (near cab partition)
- Dedicated handwash only — no food prep, no utensil washing
- Hot water required (min 100°F)
- Soap dispenser + paper towels mounted at sink
- Wash after: touching raw protein, money, phone, trash

### Zone 2 — 3-Compartment Sink
- Compartment 1: Wash (hot soapy water)
- Compartment 2: Rinse (clean hot water)
- Compartment 3: Sanitize (50–100 ppm chlorine solution)
- Test strips at sink
- Drain boards on each side

### Zone 3 — Undercounter Refrigerator
- Raw meat and eggs only
- Stored BELOW any ready-to-eat food
- Check temp AM and PM; log both readings
- Target: 38°F or below

### Zone 4 — Refrigerated Prep Rail (cold line)
- 1/3 pans: pico de gallo, diced onion, cilantro, shredded cheese, sour cream, jalapeños, salsa
- 1/6 pans: chorizo (cooked, held hot separately OR added per order), guacamole, lime wedges
- All items held at 41°F or below during service
- Replace or discard after 4 hours if not actively refrigerated

### Zone 5 — Griddle Station (main cook)
- 36"–48" commercial griddle
- Color-coded cutting boards in this zone: RED for raw meat only, GREEN for produce only
- Thermometer within arm's reach
- Keep raw zone (left side of griddle) and cooked zone (right side) clearly separated
- Never put cooked food back where raw was without cleaning

### Zone 6 — Range (2-burner)
- Heating beans, rice, sauces
- Under same hood as griddle

### Zone 7 — Service Counter / Pass Window
- Completed orders assembled here
- Squeeze bottles: salsa roja, salsa verde, crema, hot sauce
- Packaging supplies: foil, taco boats, tostada trays, napkins

### Zone 8 — Dry Storage (rear)
- Tortillas (flour and corn), spices, paper goods, spare supplies
- Off the floor; clean, dry, sealed containers

---

## Service Flow (50 orders/shift)

1. **Order called** — at window or slip
2. **Protein from raw fridge** → color-coded board → onto hot griddle
3. **Protein cooked to temp** — chorizo 160°F, eggs 160°F, carne 145°F+
4. **Tortilla warmed** — 15–30 sec on griddle or comal; kept in foil warmer
5. **Assembly at service counter** — protein + toppings from cold rail
6. **Wrap/tray and hand off** — burrito in foil, tacos in boat, tostada in flat tray
7. **Between orders** — wipe service counter with sanitizer; change gloves if touched raw protein

---

## Required Temperatures

| Item | Required Temp |
|------|--------------|
| Raw meat cold storage | 41°F or below |
| All cooked meats / poultry | 165°F internal |
| Cooked eggs (service) | 160°F (cook fully — no runny eggs on truck) |
| Hot-held cooked food | 135°F or above |
| Cold toppings / salsa | 41°F or below |
| Handwash water minimum | 100°F |
| Sanitizer solution (chlorine) | 50–100 ppm |

---

## Cross-Contamination Rules

**Raw storage order top to bottom in fridge:**
1. Ready-to-eat (cheese, salsas, prepped veg) — TOP
2. Whole cuts beef/pork
3. Ground meat (chorizo)
4. Raw poultry / eggs — BOTTOM

**Color-coded cutting boards:**
- 🔴 Red = raw meat only
- 🟡 Yellow = raw poultry/eggs only
- 🟢 Green = produce only
- Never swap or cross-use

**Handwashing triggers:**
- After touching raw protein
- After handling money or phone
- After taking out trash
- Before putting on new gloves
- Minimum 20 seconds with soap
