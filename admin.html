Settings

Live avatars / chat

Other visitors show up as avatars in the 3D scene and can type short public chat messages to each other above their avatar. This is not moderated in real time — use the off switch if someone abuses it.

AI order-taking assistant

Lives in the same chat dock as visitor chat (visitors switch modes with a button). Runs your local Ollama model to answer menu questions and can place real orders — it can only add items that actually exist on the menu, at the real price, and chat orders never carry SMS consent (that stays an explicit checkbox at checkout only).

Games

The 🎪 Midway arcade, its six minigames, the 🌭 hot dog eating contest and the 🤖 trivia bot can each be switched off. A minigame that's off loses its prop on the lot, its tab in the Midway panel and its row on the HIGH SCORES board; the trivia bot stops asking questions immediately (banked points and collections always stay); the hot dog contest loses its pill and the lawn-table tap. Visitors who already have a page open can't post scores for a game you just switched off.

Business

Online ordering

Payments

Stripe webhook to register in the Stripe dashboard → Developers → Webhooks → Add endpoint: https://food.signaturediversified.com/api/stripe/webhook, event checkout.session.completed; paste the endpoint's whsec_ here. Customers who text STOP are never texted again (self-managed opt-outs — no Messaging Service, so our webhook owns the keywords).

Promotions

Specials auto-price a menu item (optional weekdays 0=Mon..6=Sun, date window); codes are typed at checkout. Add rows, then hit Save settings.

Rewards

Rewards redeem as a cents-off discount at checkout; balances ride the phone accounts. Add rows, then Save settings.

Text alerts (SMS)

Outbound texts (order-ready + broadcasts) go through the carrier here: twilio = the newly approved direct account (credentials come from the shared sms-gateway .env unless given below), notifyhub = the old iPhone relay, gateway = the legacy local service. The admin alert phone gets an instant text the moment a visitor reports a problem through the chat assistant (chips like "Something is wrong with what I received!").

AI menu photos

Ollama writes a photo description from each listing; the local FLUX.2 klein service on your GPU renders it. Generated photos are drafts — use them as previews until you have real photos of your food.

Sampling / Quality

Settings must match the model or photos come out posterized/cartoonish. FLUX.2 klein (checkpoint blank — best realism): steps 4, sampler euler, 768–1024 px; CFG is ignored. sd_xl_turbo: steps 1–4, CFG 1.0, euler_ancestral, sgm_uniform, 512 px. v1-5: steps 25–30, CFG 6–7, dpmpp_2m, karras, 512 px.

Truck boombox & TV monitor

A boombox prop rides on the roof and plays this music; a small TV monitor on the truck's back wall plays this video on loop. Browsers block audio from autoplaying, so visitors tap a speaker button to start the music — video plays muted automatically (browsers allow that).

Jukebox (songs for points)

Visitors tap the 🎵 jukebox on the lot and spend arcade points to play a song for everyone on the lot — the same song plays on every visitor's screen at the same moment. Points deduct server-side from their phone account (like the 🏬 collectible shop). A paid play runs for the play window below, then the next queued song (up to 3 waiting) takes over. Upload songs here, set each one's title and cost, then hit Save settings.
