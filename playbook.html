Repair & Fix List

Based on photo review of vehicle. Items marked MUST FIX block county or fire inspection. BEFORE OPEN must be done before service. SOON = within 60 days of opening.

ELECTRICAL — Cab & Chassis

MUST FIX Exposed/loose wiring under cab dash

Hanging blue butt-connector wires and white plug visible under dashboard. Toggle switches have hand-written labels. Fire marshal will note any exposed conductors in the cab.
Action: Route and loom all hanging wires; secure with wire ties; label switches with legible permanent labels. Verify all cab grounds are tight.

MUST FIX Awning light panels — no lens covers

4 fluorescent tubes are bare (no diffuser/lens), dirty, and caulk around fixture is cracked/peeling. (User confirmed these need rewiring.)
Action: Install replacement LED strip lights with weather-rated fixture covers (or swap tubes for LED strips + cover panels). Re-caulk mounting seam with food-grade silicone. Verify on GFCI circuit.

BEFORE OPEN Speaker wiring

Head unit wiring harness photographed but speakers not connected. User noted wiring speakers alongside awning lights.
Action: Run speaker wire through loom alongside light wiring; connect to head-unit harness (Yellow=+12V, Black=GND, Red=ACC). Secure inside conduit so no bare wires visible in food area.

MUST FIX Service window — gas strut loose wires

Red and blue wires taped along service-window gas strut; loose black wires with exposed butt connector hanging at window opening. County health inspector and fire marshal both flag loose wires in food prep area.
Action: Secure all wires in loom or raceway away from food-contact surfaces and customer window opening.

MUST FIX Frayed wire bundle — refrigeration controls

Frayed woven sleeve exposing colored conductors at refrigeration control panel (Ranco controller area).
Action: Replace sleeve with split-loom conduit; secure all conductors; verify no bare copper exposed.

BATTERY BANK

MUST FIX Corroded battery terminals + broken tray

Heavy green/white corrosion on at least 2 terminals; broken battery-tray edge; no hold-down strap visible; debris including a 3-prong plug in the tray.
Action: Clean terminals with baking soda solution + wire brush; apply anti-corrosion spray; replace broken tray or repair with metal patch; install hold-down strap (fire code requires secured batteries); remove all foreign debris.

BEFORE OPEN Rusted junction box / electrical box — refrigeration bay

Heavily rusted electrical box at refrigeration controls. Corrosion can compromise connections inside.
Action: Open box; inspect terminals; treat or replace box if rust is through-wall; ensure IP-rated cover latches properly.

REFRIGERATION UNIT

BEFORE OPEN Compressor shell rust + performance verification

Condensing unit (AKA4460YXA / Copeland-style hermetic) has heavy rust on lower shell. Blue filter-drier and sight glass present. Unit may be functional but must hold food at required temperatures.
Action: Have a refrigeration tech check refrigerant charge, verify sight glass is clear (not bubbling), confirm unit holds 41°F or below. Replace filter-drier if last service unknown. Paint or treat shell rust to prevent further degradation. This is a county requirement — unit must demonstrate it holds temp.

SERVICE WINDOW

MUST FIX Plexiglass / barrier installation

User confirmed plexiglass is on order. County health code requires an approved barrier between food prep area and customers. Existing window must have a self-closing cover (the hinged plexi panels visible in photos).
Action: Install ordered plexiglass panels. Ensure hinges are not rusted (replace the 2 rusted hinges visible on existing plexi panels). Panel must be openable only from inside. Max opening 324 sq in per Maricopa County rules.

MUST FIX Rusty hinges on service window panels

2 clearly rusted hinges on existing hinged plexi panels.
Action: Replace with stainless steel hinges. Food-zone hardware must be non-corroding.

MUST FIX Screen required on service window

Maricopa County requires a screen on any opening to the exterior (pest exclusion). No screen visible in photos.
Action: Install 16-mesh or finer screen on service window, mounted so it can be used when the plexiglass is open. Magnetic or sliding screen works.

INTERIOR FOOD AREA

MUST FIX Griddle — rust/residue, wood board on counter

Griddle surface shows rust-colored residue. Dark wood plank on counter observed — wood is a prohibited surface in food prep areas (porous, cannot be sanitized per Maricopa County food code).
Action: Deep-clean and re-season griddle; if rust is pitted through, replace griddle plate. Remove all wood items from food prep surfaces. Replace with stainless or approved NSF-listed material only.

MUST FIX Personal items / non-food items in food cabinets

Personal drink cups, wallet-type item, and tools (rusted hinge brackets, fryer-basket handle, scraper) stored in equipment wells and cabinets that will be food areas. Health inspector will flag immediately.
Action: Remove all personal items. Designate separate locked storage for tools/personal. Dedicate all stainless wells and cabinets to food-only use.

BEFORE OPEN Stagnant water / residue in wells

Dirty water and residue visible in at least one well cover area; brown debris flakes in a stainless basin.
Action: Full clean and sanitize of all wells and basins. Remove old water. Ensure drain hoses are functional and connected.

BEFORE OPEN Peeling paint/adhesive on walls

Blue-painted wall around service window has peeling paint and adhesive residue. Peeling surfaces cannot be sanitized.
Action: Strip loose paint/adhesive; repaint with food-safe, light-colored, washable paint (or apply stainless or FRP panels). All interior walls in food zone must be smooth, light-colored, and easily cleanable.

MUST FIX Bare copper wire coil in food area

Bare copper wire coil hanging on pipe inside the main food compartment (visible near serving window area).
Action: Remove all loose wire from food prep area.

Permitting Roadmap

Complete these in order. Do not skip ahead — county plan review must happen before you operate. All fees verified from official sources as of mid-2026.

STEP-BY-STEP IN ORDER

  1. Form Your LLC $50 AZ ACC filing fee File online at azcc.gov → Corporations Division. Takes 3–5 business days. You need an LLC name, a statutory agent ($0 if you are your own agent), and an AZ address. Your EIN from IRS.gov is free and instant (call or online). Get both before applying for any license.
  2. Arizona Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT) License $12 state fee Apply at AZTaxes.gov. Select "Restaurant" under retail. This is your state sales tax license. Combined Mesa rate = 8.30% on food sales (effective Jan 1, 2026). You must have this before the city license.
  3. Food Handler Cards — All Food Workers ~$15–25 per person Maricopa County requires a Food Handler Card for everyone who handles food. Take the online course at foodhandlers.com or maricopa.gov/eh (about 90 min). The Certified Food Manager (ServSafe or equivalent) is required for the permit holder. ServSafe exam: $15 online or ~$36 in-person.
  4. Sign Commissary Agreement $0 form fee (commissary rental separate) Maricopa County requires a signed Commissary Agreement on file before issuing a mobile food permit. Must be an inspected, licensed commissary. See Commissary tab for options. Get the agreement signed by the commissary operator before you submit county paperwork.
  5. Maricopa County Mobile Food Permit — Plan Review $165 new/change-of-ownership fee Submit to Maricopa County Environmental Services (EHS): maricopa.gov/ehs. You are a Type III (cook from raw on truck). Submit: completed plan review application, commissary agreement, menu, equipment list, floor plan sketch of truck with equipment placement, proof of certified food manager. They will schedule an on-site inspection after review. Phone: (602) 506-6616.
  6. Maricopa County On-Site Health Inspection Included in $165 Inspector comes to the truck. All repairs on Tab 1 marked MUST FIX must be done before this inspection. They verify: food temps, equipment NSF-listed, no wood surfaces, proper handwash sink, 3-compartment sink, hot/cold running water, pest exclusion screens, service window cover, clean interior. Pass = county permit issued.
  7. Mesa Mobile Food Vendor License $50 initial + $20/yr renewal HB2118 that would have eliminated this was vetoed June 19, 2026. Mesa still requires it. Apply at Mesa City Hall, 20 E. Main St, or online at mesaaz.gov. Bring: county permit, TPT license, LLC docs, EIN. Must renew annually. Phone: (480) 644-2351.
  8. Mesa Fire Department Inspection Call for current fee — typically $0–100 Required per 2018 IFC Section 319 for mobile food units with cooking equipment. They inspect: fire suppression system (K-Class, UL300 listed), all cooking equipment clearances, LP gas connections (if gas), electrical, ventilation hood. Schedule AFTER county permit is in hand. Call Mesa Fire at (480) 644-2642. If you have an Ansul or similar system, have it serviced (tag must show within 6 months).
  9. AZ Dept of Revenue — Register for Withholding (if paying employees) $0 At AZTaxes.gov, register for employer withholding tax. If it is only you and family (sole member LLC / partnership), may not apply immediately — confirm with your CPA.

FLOW SEQUENCE DURING SERVICE

  1. Order taken At service window or written slip — call out to cook station
  2. Protein pulled from raw fridge Using color-coded board; immediately onto hot griddle. Never touch cooked food after touching raw.
  3. Protein cooked to temp Chorizo: 160°F internal; eggs: cook to 160°F (hard cooked for food safety); carne: per county requirements
  4. Tortilla warmed Comal or griddle; 15–30 seconds per side; kept in foil-lined insulated tortilla warmer
  5. Assembly at service counter Protein + toppings from cold rail → burrito/taco/tostada built; cheese, onion, cilantro, salsa added
  6. Wrap/tray and hand off Burrito wrapped in foil; tacos in taco boat; tostada in flat tray; passed through window to customer
  7. After each order — wipe and sanitize Wipe service counter with sanitizer solution; change gloves if touched raw protein
